## Professional bio

I am a senior software engineer with a strong track record on backend-heavy systems, product delivery, and operational maturity. My work tends to sit at the intersection of **clear architecture**, **measurable reliability**, and **shipping**—especially when legacy systems, ambiguous requirements, or team constraints make “just rewrite it” a bad default.

I am most effective where ownership is explicit: tracing problems across services, tightening feedback loops (tests, observability, deploys), and making tradeoffs legible to stakeholders before they become expensive surprises.

- <span class="text-ink font-normal underline decoration-accent underline-offset-4">Distributed systems & platforms</span> — boundaries, failure modes, idempotency, and gradual migration paths.
- <span class="text-ink font-normal underline decoration-accent underline-offset-4">Performance and cost awareness</span> — profiling judiciously, fixing what users feel, avoiding premature optimization.

- <span class="text-ink font-normal underline decoration-accent underline-offset-4">Technical leadership</span> — reviews that raise the bar, pairing, and documentation that survives contact with production.

## Experience highlights

- Led delivery of internal platforms where **data consistency and access patterns** directly affected how teams shipped features.
- Drove reliability work where the win was fewer customer-visible incidents and **faster recovery**, not buzzwords.
- Partnered across product and ops to prioritize engineering bets with **articulated downside** when we chose speed over polish (and vice versa).
